## Methodological notes

Arpen counts civil-registry births, which is a **flow** measurement, not a
stock measurement. Comparing it directly to IBGE's *Nomes no Brasil*
(whole-population stock) is an apples-to-oranges comparison; they are
different targets and the sampler models them separately.
